# Zhumeng Usage Policy

**Effective and last updated: July 23, 2026**

This Policy applies to all Zhumeng consoles, APIs, models, search functions, tools, and related services. You must also follow applicable upstream-provider policies; the stricter restriction controls where requirements differ.

## 1. Prohibited Uses

You may not use the Service to conduct, facilitate, instruct, or materially assist:

- illegal activity, sanctions or export-control evasion, money laundering, fraud, theft, scams, phishing, spam, false identity, or impersonation;
- child sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, human trafficking, sexual violence, or inappropriate conduct involving minors;
- terrorism, violent extremism, credible threats of harm, or development, acquisition, or deployment of weapons of mass destruction;
- unauthorized intrusion, credential theft, malware, ransomware, DDoS, service disruption, access-control bypass, or concealment of attack origin;
- unauthorized collection, inference, sale, or disclosure of sensitive personal data, doxxing, persistent tracking, invasive surveillance, or restricted biometrics;
- infringement of intellectual property, privacy, confidentiality, personality, or other third-party rights;
- evasion of model safeguards or Zhumeng/upstream quota, territory, rate, concurrency, identity, billing, or abuse controls;
- deceptive influence operations, fake engagement, coordinated inauthentic accounts, election interference, or deceptive publication while impersonating a real person;
- employment, housing, education, credit, insurance, healthcare, law-enforcement, or other high-impact automated decisions without qualified human review, notice, and appeal;
- personalized medical, legal, or financial conclusions without appropriate qualifications and human review, or guarantees that outputs are accurate, safe, or profitable; or
- transactions or promotion involving prohibited or strictly regulated goods and services.

## 2. Platform and Credential Abuse

You may not resell, share, or publish API keys; mass-register accounts; exploit promotions; reopen after chargebacks; generate artificial traffic; probe or exploit systems you do not own; scrape the console; reverse engineer underlying models; build a competing credential market; or interfere with other users.

## 3. Security Research

Defensive research, vulnerability validation, malware analysis, and education are permitted only with explicit authorization from the asset owner and within a controlled scope. Research may not access unrelated data, create persistent impact, expand privileges, disclose unremediated vulnerabilities, or attack production users. We may require proof of authorization and a test plan.

## 4. Content and Human Oversight

You must evaluate inputs and outputs and implement appropriate content filters, age protections, access controls, logging, and human review. Do not falsely claim that AI output was created by a human or endorsed by Zhumeng or an upstream provider. Model output must not be the sole basis for high-risk decisions.

## 5. Enforcement

We may use automated detection, sampling, and human review to identify risk and may reject requests, throttle, quarantine routes, require verification, suspend API keys, freeze accounts, or terminate service. Serious or urgent risks may be addressed without notice. Where necessary, we may preserve evidence and lawfully respond to upstream providers, affected parties, or authorities.

Report suspected abuse with timestamps, request identifiers, and evidence to `admin@5566676.xyz`. A report does not guarantee a particular action or disclosure of investigative information.
